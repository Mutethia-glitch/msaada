# Kibera impact story sources

## Living on Earth / Pulitzer Center report
URL: https://www.loe.org/shows/segmentprint.html?programID=09-P13-00033&segmentID=3

The August 14, 2009 report describes Kenya Water for Health's Solar Water Disinfection (SODIS) work in Kibera. It reports that 250,000 Kibera residents were using SODIS and that diarrheal cases had decreased by 20% since the program began in 2004. The report describes household water treatment using clear plastic bottles exposed to sunlight for at least six hours, and community outreach by Kenya Water for Health staff. It does not report volunteer hours.

## Peer-reviewed KIDS evaluation
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12570634/

The BMC Public Health evaluation describes SHOFCO's Kibera Investment for Delivery of Safe Water (KIDS) intervention in Kibera's Kisumu Ndogo and Makina villages. The intervention combined safe drinking-water delivery with WASH behavior-change communication, including community meetings, household visits, demonstrations, water-kiosk-attendant training, hygiene promotion, and soap availability in public toilets. The evaluation analyzed 1,876 participants and found a 31% relative decline in diarrheal disease in intervention villages compared with comparison villages; the intervention-village prevalence declined from 33.3% at baseline to 23.5% at endline. It does not report volunteer hours.
